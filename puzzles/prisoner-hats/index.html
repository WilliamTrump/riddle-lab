The Prisoner Hats

Ten prisoners stand in a line, each in a black or white hat. They see the hats in front of them, never their own or behind them.

  • From the back, each says black or white.
  • Everyone hears every answer.
  • Name your own hat and you go free.

Prisoners can strategize beforehand. Make a plan to save as many as possible, every time

    Hints

    1. The back prisoner is a coin flip no matter what. But everyone hears their word.

    2. Can one word say something about all the hats at once?

    3. Odd or even.

    4. The back prisoner says “black” if they see an odd number of black hats. Everyone else counts the black hats they see and hear. If that doesn't match odd/even, their own hat is black. 9 of 10 go free, every time.
